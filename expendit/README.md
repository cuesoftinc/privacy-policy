# Expendit Privacy Notice

**Effective date: 6 November 2026**

This notice covers **Expendit** at **expendit.cuesoft.io**: the app where
you record income and expenses, import bank statements and receipts, and
estimate the tax you owe.

Cuesoft is the controller of what Expendit holds at expendit.cuesoft.io, as
set out in the [privacy policy](../): Cuesoft Inc. and Cuesoft Nigeria
Limited, as joint controllers, reachable at
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).

Expendit is open-source software. If you, or an organisation you work
with, run your own copy, whoever runs it is the controller of the data it
holds, and this notice does not describe it.

## Who Can Use Expendit

Expendit is for people **18 and older**. See [children](../rights/children/).

## Signing In

- **What we collect:** you sign in with Google, the only way in. Expendit
  receives from Google your Google account identifier, email address and
  name.
- **Session:** a cookie that scripts on the page cannot read, valid for up
  to 24 hours.
- **Legal basis:** performing the contract you enter when you create an
  account (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), and the equivalent contractual basis under
  the NDPA).

## Your Books

- **What we collect:** the organisations you keep books for (a personal
  one is created for you, and you may add companies with their name and
  registered address), the people you invite to them with their email and
  role, your categories, and your transactions: description, amount, date
  and where each came from.
- **For tax estimates:** your tax identification number, state of
  residence, company registration number, national identification number,
  annual rent and the deductions you claim, as you enter them, and the
  estimates, statements and filing drafts Expendit works out from them.
- **Who sees it:** you, and the members you invite to an organisation,
  with the role you give them.
- **Retention:** until you delete it or your account.
- **Legal basis:** performing the contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

## Importing Statements and Receipts

- **What happens:** the file you upload (a CSV, spreadsheet, PDF or image)
  is read into transactions you review before anything enters your books.
- **The file itself** is deleted within an hour of being read or
  discarded, and our storage provider keeps a recovery copy for up to 7
  more days. The import’s record (the file’s name, type and size, the rows
  it produced and a summary) is deleted 90 days after you confirm or
  discard it.
- **Legal basis:** performing the contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

## AI Processing

Expendit can use AI to read PDFs and images, suggest categories and write
a short summary of an import. It does so **only if you consent** to AI
processing; without that consent, images are refused and every other file
is read without AI.

- **What the AI receives:** the text of a PDF statement, in parts; the
  image of a receipt or statement; the description and type of each
  transaction with your category names, to suggest a category; and, for
  the summary, totals only (the number of transactions, income, expenses,
  net and the top five categories). Spreadsheets and CSV files never go to
  AI.
- **Who runs it:** **Google’s Vertex AI**, inside our own Google Cloud
  project in the United States, acting as our processor. Google does not
  use what it receives to train its models.
- **Withdrawing:** you can withdraw AI consent in your settings, and
  imports after that are read without AI. Until the app records the
  withdrawal itself, email [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and
  we will record it for you.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), and consent under NDPA
  s. 25).

## Logs and Monitoring

Our servers keep operational logs of each request’s method, address and
outcome, never the contents of your books. They are kept for no more than
**30 days**: see [retention](../handling/retention/).

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in running a secure, working
  service (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you may [object](../rights/your-rights/).

## Where Your Data Is Held

Expendit’s services and files run on **Google Cloud** in Iowa, United
States, and its databases are run for us by **Aiven** in DigitalOcean’s
San Francisco region. See [processors and platforms](../handling/processors/)
and [international transfers](../handling/transfers/).

## Your Rights in Expendit

Email [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) to get a copy of
everything Expendit holds about you, to correct it, or to delete your
account and its data. Every right, and how to exercise it, is on
[your rights](../rights/your-rights/).

**We do not sell personal data, and Expendit carries no advertising
tags.**

# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 16 August 2026**

This policy explains what personal data the Cuesoft websites collect, why,
and the rights you have over it — under Nigerian, EU/UK and US law.

It is issued by **Cuesoft Inc.** (8 The Green, Suite 300, Dover, DE 19901,
United States) and **Cuesoft Nigeria Limited** (RC1624434, 39 Alfred Rewane
Road, 3rd Floor Mulliner Towers, Ikoyi, Lagos, Nigeria) — together
"Cuesoft", "we", "us" — who act as joint data controllers for the websites
below. Under our joint-controller arrangement, Cuesoft Nigeria Limited is
the responsible entity and primary contact for processing subject to
Nigerian law, and Cuesoft Inc. for processing subject to US law; you may
exercise your rights against either entity through
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).

Signed engagements — offers of admission, internship, contractor and client
agreements, Cueprise™ licences — carry their own data terms, which govern
those relationships alongside this policy.

## The short version

- We collect only what each website needs to do its job — mostly what you
  type into an application or contact form.
- **No payment data, ever**: our websites take no payments.
- **We do not sell personal data.** One site (CueTA™) uses advertising
  tags, and only if you opt in through its consent banner — see the CueTA™
  section and the United States section for exactly what that means.
- Our AI assistant (Ace) processes what you type to it in order to answer;
  don't type sensitive personal information into a chat box.
- You can exercise your rights — access, correction, deletion and more —
  by emailing [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).

## What every Cuesoft website collects

The following applies to **cuesoft.io**, **cueta.cuesoft.io**,
**cuelabs.cuesoft.io** and **cuehire.cuesoft.io** alike:

- **Server and security logs.** Our sites are served through Cloudflare
  (edge security and delivery) and hosted on Google Firebase App Hosting.
  Standard technical data — IP address, browser, requested pages,
  timestamps — appears in short-lived infrastructure logs used for
  security, abuse prevention and reliability. Legal basis: our legitimate
  interest in operating secure websites (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Cookieless analytics.** We measure aggregate traffic with Cloudflare
  Web Analytics, a script that uses **no cookies**, no persistent
  identifiers and no cross-site tracking.
- **Ace, the assistant.** If you chat with Ace, the messages you type are
  processed by our AI platform provider to generate an answer.
  Conversations live in your browser session and are not linked to an
  account (there are no accounts), and under our agreement with our AI
  provider, chat inputs are not used to train AI models. Please do not
  submit sensitive personal data through the chat. Legal basis: our
  legitimate interest in providing the assistant you choose to use
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Except as stated per-site below, the websites set **no cookies** and load
**no advertising scripts and no third-party analytics** beyond the
cookieless Cloudflare measurement described above.

## Site by site

### cuesoft.io — the corporate site

- No forms. The site collects nothing you don't type into Ace.
- The media section offers a Spotify player for The CueShow™ that loads
  **only if you click it**; once loaded, Spotify may set its own cookies
  under [Spotify's privacy policy](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/).
  Blog posts are fetched from Medium by our servers at build time — your
  browser never talks to Medium on our site.

### cueta.cuesoft.io — Cuesoft Talent Academy

- **Application form.** Name, email, phone number, and your programme
  intent. We use it to process your application, run your free consultation
  call and administer enrolment. Records are stored in Airtable (our forms
  database). Legal basis: steps at your request prior to a contract
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and the equivalent contractual basis under the NDPA.
  Application decisions are made by humans; we do not make solely automated
  decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
- **Advertising tags — opt-in only.** CueTA™ shows a consent banner. Only
  if you press **Accept** does the site load its advertising tags, used to
  measure and reach prospective learners:

  | Provider | Purpose | Their policy |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Google Ads | Ad measurement and remarketing | [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
  | Meta | Ad measurement and remarketing | [facebook.com/privacy/policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/) |
  | LinkedIn | Ad measurement | [linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy) |
  | X (Twitter) | Ad measurement | [x.com/en/privacy](https://x.com/en/privacy) |

  Decline and none of them load. You can withdraw at any time via the
  site's **Cookie preferences** link — withdrawal stops the tags loading
  from that point on; it does not delete cookies the platforms already set
  (you can clear those in your browser), and data those platforms already
  collected is governed by their own policies. We do not send your form
  contents (email, phone) to these advertising platforms.
- **Cohort communication.** Enrolled learners are contacted about their
  cohort via the details they provided, including WhatsApp groups for
  cohort coordination — see "Communication platforms" below.

### cuelabs.cuesoft.io — Open-source Internships

- **Application form.** Name, email, phone, GitHub username, role interest

- Accepted interns join the CueLABS™ Discord community — see
  "Communication platforms" below.
- No cookies, no advertising or third-party analytics scripts beyond the
  cookieless Cloudflare measurement described above, and no consent

### cuehire.cuesoft.io — Talent and Project Management Agency

- **Contact form.** Name, work email, company, and your brief — used to
  respond to your enquiry and scope a potential engagement, stored in
  Airtable. Legal basis: steps at your request prior to a contract
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); our legitimate interest in responding to business
  enquiries.
- No cookies, no advertising or third-party analytics scripts beyond the
  cookieless Cloudflare measurement described above, and no consent banner.

### handbook.cuesoft.io, privacy.cuesoft.io, terms.cuesoft.io

Static documentation served via GitHub Pages, so GitHub's infrastructure

## Our processors and platforms

We use a small set of service providers to run the websites, each bound by
its data processing agreement to process data only on our instructions:
**Cloudflare** (edge delivery, security, cookieless analytics), **Google**
(Firebase App Hosting infrastructure; the Google Ads tag on CueTA™ after
consent), **Airtable** (form records), **GitHub** (hosting for the
documentation sites; public API for the contributors display), and our
**AI platform provider** (generating Ace's answers — the provider may
change, and we will name the current one on request via
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io)). Only where you opt in as
described above do **Meta, LinkedIn and X** (CueTA™ advertising tags) or
**Spotify** (the click-to-load player) receive anything.

**Communication platforms.** Programme coordination happens on WhatsApp
(CueTA™ cohorts) and Discord (CueLABS™ interns). Joining a group makes
your profile name — and on WhatsApp, your phone number — visible to other
group members, and your use of those platforms is governed by their own
privacy policies. Participation in a given channel is part of how the
programme runs; tell us if you need an alternative arrangement.

We do not sell personal data to anyone.

## International transfers

We operate from Nigeria and the United States, and our processors operate
globally, so your data may be processed outside your country. For EU/UK
data, we rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum
or IDTA where the UK GDPR applies) incorporated into our processors' data
processing agreements, and on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where a
processor is certified under it. Transfers between Cuesoft Inc. and Cuesoft
Nigeria Limited take place under our intra-group arrangement applying the
same safeguards. Where the NDPA applies, transfers follow its adequacy and
safeguard provisions.

## Retention

- Form submissions (applications, enquiries): kept while we process and
  follow up; records of unsuccessful or inactive applications and enquiries
  are deleted or anonymised within **24 months** of last contact, unless a
  legal duty requires longer. Verified deletion requests are honoured
  sooner.
- Infrastructure and security logs: retained no more than **30 days**.
- Ace conversations: held in your browser session; server-side processing
  is transient.
- Data under signed engagements: retained per the engagement's terms and
  applicable law.

## Security

We apply proportionate technical and organisational safeguards: encrypted
transport (TLS) everywhere, edge protection and origin controls on our
APIs, least-privilege access to form records, two-factor authentication on
company systems, and an internal
[data-protection policy](https://handbook.cuesoft.io/policies/data-protection/)
binding everyone who works with us. No internet transmission is guaranteed
100% secure; we commit to prompt, lawful handling if something goes wrong —
see "Breach notification" below.

## Your rights

You control your personal data. Depending on your jurisdiction (detailed
below), you have the right to: **access** a copy of your data;
**correct** inaccurate data; **delete** data; **restrict** or **object to**
processing (including processing based on legitimate interests);
**portability** in a structured, machine-readable format; **withdraw
consent** at any time (without affecting prior lawful processing); and
**complain** to your supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these, email
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) with enough information to
verify you. We respond within one month; where a request is complex we may
lawfully extend and will tell you why. Exercising rights is free except
where the law permits a reasonable fee for manifestly excessive requests.

## Nigeria (NDPA 2023)

For processing subject to Nigerian law, Cuesoft Nigeria Limited is the
responsible entity under our joint-controller arrangement. You hold the
rights above under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023** and its
subsidiary instruments (including the NDPC's General Application and
Implementation Directive 2025), and you may lodge a complaint with the
**Nigeria Data Protection Commission** at
[ndpc.gov.ng](https://ndpc.gov.ng/). Where a qualifying breach occurs, we
notify the NDPC within **72 hours** of becoming aware, and notify you
without undue delay where the breach is likely to put your rights and
freedoms at risk.

## European Union, EEA and United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to our
processing of your data as described in this policy. Our legal bases are
stated per collection above (contractual steps, consent, legitimate
interests). You hold all the rights listed in "Your rights", including the
right to object to legitimate-interest processing and the right to lodge a
complaint with your local supervisory authority (or the UK ICO). Our
processing of EU/UK personal data is occasional and low-risk, and we rely
on the exemption in Article 27(2) GDPR from appointing an EU/UK
representative; you can reach the controllers directly at
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io). International transfers are
safeguarded as described above.

## United States

If you are in the United States, the privacy laws of your state may grant
you rights of access, correction, deletion and portability, which we honour
through the same contact regardless of threshold applicability.

For California residents, in CCPA/CPRA terms: we **do not sell** personal
information. On cueta.cuesoft.io only, and only if you opt in through the
consent banner, we **"share"** identifiers and internet-activity data with
the advertising platforms listed above for cross-context behavioral
advertising; you can opt out of further sharing at any time via the
**Cookie preferences** link. The tags never load without your opt-in, so if
your browser sends an opt-out preference signal (such as Global Privacy
Control) and you have not opted in, nothing is shared. We collect only the
categories described above (identifiers and internet activity), for the
purposes described above; we do not use or disclose sensitive personal
information beyond what is necessary to provide the services; and we will
not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right.

## Breach notification

If a breach of security leads to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss,
alteration or unauthorised disclosure of personal data, we will promptly
assess and contain it, notify the competent authorities where and when
required (including the NDPC within 72 hours where the NDPA applies, and
EU/UK authorities without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours
where the GDPR requires notification), and inform affected people without
undue delay where their rights and freedoms are at material risk.

## Children

Our websites and programmes are directed to people **18 and older**. We do
not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18; if you believe a
minor has provided us data, contact
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and we will delete it.

# Nigeria: NDPA 2023

For processing subject to Nigerian law, **Cuesoft Nigeria Limited**
(RC1624434) is the responsible entity under our joint-controller
arrangement.

## The framework

Our processing is governed by the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023**
(NDPA) and its subsidiary instruments, including the Nigeria Data
Protection Commission’s **General Application and Implementation Directive
(GAID) 2025**.

## Lawful bases

The NDPA recognises consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interest,
public interest and legitimate interest as lawful bases (NDPA s. 25). We
state the basis for each collection on the
[collection pages](../../collection/all-sites/): forms rest on contract
(steps at your request prior to one), except CueHIRE™ enquiries, Cueprise™ demo requests,
Storefront™ enquiries and Cuelearn™ demo requests, which rest on legitimate interest because the
agreement that may follow is with your organisation rather than with you;
infrastructure, Ace and the websites’ and documentation sites’ performance and error
monitoring on legitimate interest, and website analytics and advertising tags on consent, which you can withdraw
as easily as you gave it.

## Your rights under the NDPA

You hold the rights of a data subject under Part VI of the NDPA,
including: to be informed (this policy); access; correction; deletion;
restriction; objection; data portability; to withdraw consent; and not to
be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similar effect,
which [we do not make](../../rights/your-rights/). Exercise any of them
through [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) as described on the
[rights page](../../rights/your-rights/).

## Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission**:
[ndpc.gov.ng](https://ndpc.gov.ng/). We would appreciate the chance to
resolve your concern first, but you do not need our permission: the right
is yours.

## Breach notification

Where a qualifying breach occurs, we notify the **NDPC within 72 hours**
of becoming aware, and notify you without undue delay where the breach is
likely to put your rights and freedoms at risk: see
[breach notification](../../handling/breach/).

## Transfers out of Nigeria

Cross-border transfers follow the NDPA’s adequacy and safeguard
provisions and the NDPC’s implementing instruments: see
[international transfers](../../handling/transfers/).

## Registration status

The NDPC designates a **data controller of major importance** on any one of
three independent grounds: processing the personal data of more than 200
data subjects in six months; carrying out commercial ICT services on a
device that holds another person’s data; or processing personal data as an
organisation or service provider in one of thirteen named sectors. Those
sectors include education, and the Commission’s own classification of
designated entities names corporate training service providers.

Whether Cuesoft is designated on any of those grounds is under review. This
page will state the outcome, and where designation applies it will name our
registration and our designated data protection officer. Questions in the
meantime: [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).

Our data protection officer is one of Cuesoft’s founders, reached at
[privacy@cuesoft.io](mailto:privacy@cuesoft.io).

## Sensitive personal data

The NDPA restricts the processing of sensitive personal data, such as health
information, biometric and genetic data, race or ethnic origin, religious
belief and political opinion, unless a stated condition applies, such as the
data subject’s explicit consent. We do not ask for it on our websites. Where a
product handles data that may be sensitive, we rely on your explicit consent,
which you can withdraw: Apparule asks for consent before your first body
photo, Expendit asks before its AI reads your statements, and a Cuelearn™
Teacher’s cloned voice is made only with that Teacher’s explicit consent.

The NDPA counts biometric data as sensitive only when it is processed to
identify a person uniquely (s. 65). None of the following is processed that
way:

- **Identity documents on account.cuesoft.io:** our staff look at them to
  confirm who you are. Nothing reads your face or matches it against
  anything. They rest on the contract basis in the table above.
- **Apparule body photos and measurements:** they are used only to work out
  your measurements and are never used to recognise who you are. If a photo
  happens to show something about your health, the explicit consent you
  give before your first capture covers it (s. 30(1)(a)).
- **A Cuelearn™ Teacher’s cloned voice:** it is made to read lessons aloud,
  not to identify the Teacher, and only with the Teacher’s explicit consent,
  which also meets s. 30(1)(a).

## Children and the NDPA

The NDPA treats a person under 18 as a child and requires the consent of a
parent or guardian before a child’s personal data is processed. Our services
are for people 18 and older, with two exceptions: see
[children](../../rights/children/). On Apparule, a parent’s or guardian’s
agreement is required for anyone under 18. On the Cuelearn™ platform, the
Institution that enrols a Learner under 18 is the controller and is
responsible for the parent’s or guardian’s consent.

## When Cuesoft is the processor

For Institution data on Cuelearn™ and Licensee data on Cueprise™, the
Institution or Licensee is the data controller under the NDPA, and Cuesoft
Nigeria Limited acts as its data processor, on its instructions, under the
data terms of the licensing agreement. We tell the controller of a breach
without undue delay so it can meet its own duty to the NDPC and to the people
affected: see [breach notification](../../handling/breach/).

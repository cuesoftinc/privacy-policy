# Cuelearn™ Privacy Notice

Cuelearn™ is the learning platform we sell to institutions: academies,
schools, universities and training providers. This page covers the
**marketing site** at cuelearn.cuesoft.io, where you can read about it and
ask for a demo, and, from [The Cuelearn™ platform](#the-cuelearn-platform)
below, the **platform** itself, where teachers and learners sign in at
canvas.cuesoft.io.

## Demo request form

- **What:** your name, work email, the name of your institution, the kind of
  institution you picked from the list (academy, school, university,
  training provider or other), and anything you added in the message box. We
  also record when you accepted the terms, and the country Cloudflare’s edge
  places you in: derived from your IP address as you submit rather than
  asked for on the form, and stored as the country name, not the address
  itself.
- **Why:** to reply to your request and arrange the demo.
- **Where:** records are stored in Airtable, our forms database.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in responding to the business
  enquiry you sent us (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and the equivalent
  legitimate-interest basis under the NDPA; you may
  [object](../rights/your-rights/) at any time. An agreement that follows
  is between Cuesoft and your institution rather than with you: if you
  enquired as an employee or representative you are usually not a party to
  it, so contract performance cannot become the basis for your own name and
  work email. Where an agreement is signed with you as an individual,
  processing under it rests on that contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Confirmation:** we email you a receipt, copied to
  [cuelearn@cuesoft.io](mailto:cuelearn@cuesoft.io); see
  [what every site collects](../collection/all-sites/).

## Performance and error monitoring

This site loads **Datadog**’s browser monitoring. It is operational
telemetry, so it does not wait for the consent banner, and declining the
banner does not stop it: it measures how quickly a page loads and records
the errors it hits, so that we can keep the pages working.

- **What Datadog receives:** the page addresses you visit and the page
  that referred you, load and resource timings, your clicks (which
  element, never anything you type), script errors and failed requests,
  your browser, device and operating system, and your IP address, from
  which it derives an approximate location. Nothing links a session to
  your name or email. No session is recorded as a replay, with one
  exception: if a page fails to render correctly, Datadog records that
  session as a replay so we can see what went wrong. A replay shows the
  page as it appeared to you, and the contents of form fields are masked.
- **Cookie:** one first-party session cookie, `_dd_s_v2`, holding a random
  session identifier that lapses after 15 minutes of inactivity (4 hours
  at most).
- **Role and retention:** Datadog acts as our **processor**, on our
  instructions, and keeps the data in the United States for no more than
  **30 days**, under the safeguards on the
  [transfers page](../handling/transfers/); see
  [processors and platforms](../handling/processors/) and
  [retention](../handling/retention/).
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the pages working
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you may [object](../rights/your-rights/) at any
  time.

## What this site does not do

It takes no payment and quotes no price, and it offers no signup or trial:
an institution asks for a demo, and Cuelearn™ is licensed under a separate
written agreement. Apart from the
[performance and error monitoring](#performance-and-error-monitoring) above, nothing
loads before you accept, and the cookieless Cloudflare measurement described
in [what every site collects](../collection/all-sites/) stays outside the consent gate
because it sets no cookie.

## The Cuelearn™ platform

Everything from here covers the platform at **canvas.cuesoft.io**, where
teachers and learners sign in. Everything above covers the marketing site.

The [Cuelearn™ Terms](https://terms.cuesoft.io/cuelearn/) govern how the
platform may be used. In this section, an **Institution** is the school,
academy, university, training provider or other organisation that licenses
Cuelearn™, and **Institution data** is everything it and its users put into
the platform or generate on it.

### How Cuelearn™ is deployed

Cuesoft operates the platform. The application runs on Google Cloud Run,
records are kept in a PostgreSQL database with a Redis cache, and files are
kept in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket that only the platform reads.
A course's cover picture is the exception: the platform serves it without a
sign-in, so it can show in the console, on course cards and in emails. The
platform checks its licence against Cuesoft's licensing service.

### Signing in

Depending on how the Institution sets it up, you sign in with a one-time code
sent to your email or by WhatsApp, with a Google account, with the
Institution's own single sign-on, or with your Cuesoft account.

- **One-time codes:** the code goes to the email address or phone number on
  your account. If no account matches, nothing is sent, so the platform does
  not reveal who has an account.
- **Google:** the platform receives your Google account's identifier, your
  verified email address and, if your account has none, your profile
  picture.
- **Single sign-on:** your Institution's own identity provider confirms who
  you are, under the Institution's own terms and privacy notice.
- **Cuesoft account:** the platform asks Cuesoft's account service whose
  session it is, and receives your account identifier.

### Roles

- **The institution decides who uses it.** The school or institution that
  licenses Cuelearn™ decides which learners and teachers use the platform.
  It is responsible for the consent of a parent or guardian for any learner
  under 18.
- **Cuesoft processes on the institution's behalf.** The institution is the
  controller of learner and teacher data, and Cuesoft processes it for the
  institution, on its instructions, under the data terms of the licensing
  agreement.
- **Requests go to your institution.** If you are a learner, a teacher or a
  parent or guardian, the institution's own privacy notice governs the
  records about you, and rights requests about them go to the institution.
  We refer any request we receive to it, and help it answer.

| Data                                                                                                               | Controller      | Processor |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------- | --------- |
| Institution data: accounts, courses, coursework, marks, live sessions, messages, files and reports about learners  | The Institution | Cuesoft   |
| Relationship data: the Institution's contacts, contracts, invoices and support correspondence                      | Cuesoft         |           |

### AI

AI-drafted lessons, quizzes and marking run on Google Gemini, through Vertex
AI and the Gemini API. Prompts are not used to train models.

- **What is sent:** the prompt, and the course content or learner answers
  the feature needs: for example a lesson's topic to draft it, or a
  learner's answers to mark them.
- **Voice-overs:** a lesson can be read aloud in one of Google's ready-made
  Gemini voices, or in the teacher's cloned voice described below.
- **Ace:** Ace answers questions and can propose changes, such as a new
  lesson title. It makes a change only after you confirm it.
- **A teacher checks:** a teacher can review any marked attempt, add a note
  and correct the score. Marks, grades and progression are the
  Institution's decisions; Cuesoft makes no decision about a learner, and
  nothing on the platform makes a solely automated decision with legal or
  similarly significant effects for a learner.
- **Who is involved:** Google acts as a sub-processor, listed in the
  [table below](#sub-processors).

### A teacher's voice

A teacher's cloned voice is made only with that teacher's explicit consent.
It is deleted when the teacher withdraws that consent.

### Exams

Invigilated exams run on Google Meet. Cameras must be on for the whole
sitting.

- **What the camera shows:** the learner's live video and audio pass through
  Google Meet so the invigilator can see the sitting. The platform itself
  does not store that video.
- **Why:** so the Institution can confirm that the right person sat the exam
  under its rules.
- **Legal basis:** the Institution decides the basis, as the controller. A
  learner who cannot keep a camera on tells the Institution before the
  sitting.

### Live sessions

Live classes run on Google Meet too. The platform creates each session,
sends the invitations, and keeps the session's title, times, link and
attendance: who joined, and the teacher's own attendance marks. Where a
teacher adds a recording to a session, the platform keeps its link so
learners can watch it. Recording a session, and telling the people in it, are
the Institution's responsibility.

### Parent and guardian reports

Parents and guardians receive weekly reports about their learner.

- **What is in a report:** a summary of the learner's week on the platform,
  sent to the parent or guardian the Institution has linked to that learner.
- **How it is sent:** by email, by WhatsApp, or both, as the parent or
  guardian chooses. Each report email carries a way to stop the reports.
- **What is kept:** whether reports are on, the channel, and a record of
  each delivery: the week, whether it was sent, held or failed, and the
  reason for a failure.

### Certificates

When a learner completes a course, the platform asks Cuesoft's certificate
register, which runs on Cuesoft's Cueprise™ platform, to issue a
certificate. The register keeps the holder's name, the course title, the
code and the issue date. Anyone with the code can check the certificate on
Cuesoft's public verification page, which shows whether it is valid or
revoked. A learner chooses who to give the code to.

### Lesson videos on YouTube

A teacher can turn a lesson into a video. Cuesoft renders it on Google Cloud
and keeps the video and its captions as files on the platform. The
Institution can then publish it to the Institution's own YouTube channel as
public, unlisted or private, and the platform keeps the video's YouTube
address and status. When a newer video replaces an older one, the older one
is made private on YouTube. Once published, YouTube holds the video under
the Institution's channel and Google's own privacy policy; the Institution
decides what goes into a video and who can see it.

### Community and messages

- **Community:** a learner can see other learners who share a course and
  send them a connection request with a short message. The other learner
  accepts or declines it.
- **Inbox:** the platform tells you about your own work, such as an
  enrolment that starts, a new mark or a meeting, and staff can send a
  message to a user of the same Institution.

### Files

Learners and staff can attach files to their work. Files are kept in the
private bucket described above, and the platform serves them only through
its own links. The platform accepts only the file types and sizes it lists.

### When Cuesoft is the processor

For Institution data, Cuesoft processes **only on the Institution's
documented instructions**, under the data terms of the licensing agreement:

1. To provide, support, secure and maintain the platform, and for nothing
   else. Cuesoft does not mine Institution data, does not use it for
   advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI models.
2. Access inside Cuesoft is limited to the people who need it for their
   role, and they are bound by
   [confidentiality](https://handbook.cuesoft.io/policies/confidentiality/)
   and [data-protection](https://handbook.cuesoft.io/policies/data-protection/)
   obligations.
3. Sub-processors are engaged under contracts that impose the same
   protections. The table below names them; each licensing agreement
   confirms which are active and how the Institution is told of changes.
4. On termination or expiry, Institution data is returned or deleted as the
   agreement provides.
5. Cuesoft assists the Institution with requests from the people the data
   is about, security incidents and impact assessments concerning
   Institution data, as the agreement and applicable law require.

### When Cuesoft is the controller

For **relationship data**, the Institution contacts, contracts, invoices and
support correspondence involved in running the licensing relationship,
Cuesoft is the controller. **Legal basis:** where the Institution's
counterparty is an individual, performance of the licensing contract (GDPR
Art. 6(1)(b)); for the staff and representatives of an Institution, who are
usually not parties to the contract themselves, our legitimate interest in
managing the relationship their Institution entered into (Art. 6(1)(f));
and, for records kept beyond the relationship, compliance with legal
obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). The NDPA's equivalent bases apply in the same
way.

Cuesoft is also the controller of the **service telemetry** needed to run
and secure the platform:

- **Server-side:** request traces, error reports and operational logs.
- **In the browser:** Datadog's browser monitoring measures how the
  platform's pages load and records the errors they hit. In production it
  can record a sample of sessions as replays to debug a fault, with the text
  on the screen and what you type masked.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in securing, supporting and
  improving the platform (Art. 6(1)(f)). Because learners can be under 18,
  replays mask what is on the screen, and telemetry is never used to profile
  a learner or for advertising.

Service telemetry is kept for no more than **30 days**, as the
[retention page](../handling/retention/) sets for infrastructure and
security logs.

### Children

Learners can be under 18 where their Institution enrols them.

- The Institution decides whether a child uses the platform, and is
  responsible for the consent of a parent or guardian and for any other
  consent the law of the child's country requires.
- Cuesoft processes a child's data only for the Institution, only to
  provide the platform, and never for advertising, marketing or profiling.
- The platform carries no advertising tags.
- A parent or guardian who wants to see, correct or delete a child's
  records asks the Institution; Cuesoft helps it answer. See
  [children](../rights/children/).

### Security and breach

The platform is operated under the safeguards on the
[security page](../handling/security/), plus any platform-specific measures
stated in each licensing agreement. Security incidents affecting
Institution data are notified to the affected Institution without undue
delay, with the detail the Institution needs for its own legal obligations:
see [breach notification](../handling/breach/).

### Retention

Data on the platform is kept as the institution instructs.

- **While the agreement runs:** Institution data stays until the Institution
  or its users delete it, or the Institution tells us to delete it.
- **When the agreement ends:** Institution data is returned or deleted as
  the agreement provides.
- **A teacher's cloned voice:** deleted when the teacher withdraws consent.
- **Service telemetry:** no more than **30 days**.
- **Relationship data:** as the [retention page](../handling/retention/)
  and the agreement provide.

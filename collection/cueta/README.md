# cueta.cuesoft.io: Cuesoft Talent Academy

## Enrolment and Payment

You enrol in a CueTA™ course from its page on cueta.cuesoft.io. Enrolling is
a paid subscription. The site has no application form and takes no
applications. You sign in to your Cuesoft account at account.cuesoft.io,
check the price, and pay with a card saved on your account.

- **How you enrol:** the site asks the Cuesoft platform that runs your
  account and courses for a quote, then creates an order, then asks the
  platform to charge the card you chose. If Paystack has not finished that
  payment, the site asks the platform to confirm it with the payment
  reference. The same request sent twice takes one payment.
- **Course pages:** the price, duration, outline, deliverables and timeline
  on a course page are read from the platform’s course catalogue, which the
  site’s server asks for without a sign-in. It holds course information and
  no personal data.
- **What we collect:** your name and email address from your account; the
  course and plan you chose; the price, with any discount and tax; and the
  order and invoice records: order number, invoice number, date, total, and
  whether payment succeeded or failed, with the reason given for a failure
  (for example declined, insufficient funds, expired card or incorrect
  security code). For the card on file we keep and show only the card brand,
  the last four digits, the expiry month and year, and whether it is your
  default card.
- **Why:** to take your payment, enrol you in the course, send your
  receipt, keep your invoices, open a new week of content each week while
  your subscription is active, and issue your certificate.
- **Adding a card:** the card number, expiry date and security code are typed
  only into Paystack’s own window, never into cueta.cuesoft.io, which has no
  field for them. Before the window opens, the platform starts a card
  verification checkout of 50 naira, and the platform refunds that charge.
  Paystack’s script loads only when you press Add Card. We do not send the
  card to the advertising platforms listed below. What the platform asks and
  keeps about each verification is on the
  [Cards section of the account page](../account/#cards).
- **Who takes the payment:** **Paystack** is our card payment provider. It
  processes the card payment for your order under its own security
  certification and its own privacy notice: see
  [processors and platforms](../../handling/processors/).
- **Where:** your account, enrolment, order and invoice records are held on
  the Cuesoft platform that runs your account and courses.
- **Legal basis:** performing the contract you enter by enrolling (GDPR
  Art. 6(1)(b), and the equivalent contractual basis under the NDPA);
  keeping invoice and tax records is a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
- **Receipt:** we email your receipt to the address on your account.
- **If payment fails:** your card is not charged for that order, and the
  page tells you the reason. You can try again, or use a different card, from
  your account. If a payment on your subscription does not go through, your
  account stays open for now, new content waits, and your access to your
  courses can pause until the payment is made: update your card or pay the
  invoice and it opens again.
- **Stopping:** you can cancel your subscription from your subscription page at
  any time. That stops future charges, and your courses stay open until the end
  of the period you have paid for. You can add another card and make it your
  default; to have a saved card deleted, use the
  [rights page](../../rights/your-rights/). Invoice and tax records are kept
  for as long as the law requires: see
  [retention](../../handling/retention/).
- **Certificate:** your certificate is issued once your final payment is
  made and you have completed the course. Issuing it is described under
  [processors and platforms](../../handling/processors/). Anyone with a
  certificate code can check it on the site’s Verify a Certificate page. The
  page asks the platform about the code and shows the holder’s name, the
  title, the tier if there is one, the date and whether the certificate is
  valid or revoked.
- **Advertising platforms:** the only event we send them is the one for a
  paid enrolment, described below. We do not send them your card details,
  name or email address. As on every page, if you
  accept the banner, they receive the page views described below, which on
  these pages include the page address.

## Analytics and Advertising Tags: Opt-In Only

All nine Cuesoft websites that carry them (CueTA™, CueLABS™, CueHIRE™,
Cueprise™, Storefront™, Cuelearn™, cuesoft.io, clients.cuesoft.io and
account.cuesoft.io)
use analytics and advertising tags, and on all of them the tags are gated
behind a consent banner. The documentation sites carry none of them, only
the performance and error monitoring described on
[their page](../docs-sites/). Only if you press **Accept** does the site
load them:

| Google Ads       | Ad measurement and remarketing                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)                 |

| LinkedIn         | Ad measurement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | [linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy) |
| X (Twitter)      | Ad measurement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | [x.com/en/privacy](https://x.com/en/privacy)                                       |

- **What they receive:** a cookie or device identifier, your IP address,
  the page URLs you visit on this site and the referring URL, plus basic
  device and browser metadata and the page-view events themselves. Google
  Analytics uses the IP address in passing to derive an approximate
  location and then discards it: the address itself is not stored, and the
  location is what is kept. Google Analytics keeps
  this for **14 months**: see [retention](../../handling/retention/).
- **One event beyond page views:**
  when an enrolment payment succeeds and the enrolment confirmed page opens,
  **Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and X** are told that it happened, so
  they can credit the ad you arrived from. Google Ads receives a
  conversion, Meta receives a Lead event, and LinkedIn and X each receive
  the conversion event set up for this site. We send it once for each paid
  order, and only on the live site, only after you press Accept, and never
  while your browser sends Global Privacy Control. Google Analytics is not
  told: it receives the page views described above and nothing more. The
  event records the fact of the enrolment and nothing else: not your name, not
  your email, not your phone number, not the course and not what you paid.
- **If you are signed in to Google:** we have Google signals switched on,
  so where you are signed in to a Google account that has Ads
  Personalisation enabled, Google may connect this visit to that account
  and combine it with what Google already holds there: location, search
  history, YouTube history and activity on sites that partner with Google.
  What that does, precisely: it lets us see age, gender and interest
  summaries, and it lets Google build advertising audiences that can reach
  you across your devices. It does **not** merge your devices together in
  our own reports (since February 2024 Google signals is not part of how
  Analytics counts users), so we do not see your phone and your laptop as
  one person. And Google uses the visit for its own ads personalisation as
  well as for our measurement. You control it on Google’s side,
  independently of us, at
  [myadcenter.google.com](https://myadcenter.google.com) and
  [Google’s activity controls](https://myaccount.google.com/activitycontrols):
  switching Ads Personalisation off there stops it for every site, not
  just ours. Declining our banner prevents it entirely.
- **Decline and none of them load.** The site works identically either
  way.
- **Withdraw any time** via the site’s **Cookie preferences** link.
  Withdrawal stops the tags loading from that point on; it does not delete
  cookies already set (you can clear those in your browser). What happens
  to data already collected differs by provider: Google Analytics holds it
  as our **processor**, on our instructions, so the
  [rights page](../../rights/your-rights/) reaches it. The exception is what
  Google signals feeds into Google’s own ads personalisation, which Google
  controls rather than us: manage that in your
  [Google account](https://myadcenter.google.com). The four advertising
  platforms hold what they collected as **independent businesses** under
  their own policies: see
  [processors and platforms](../../handling/processors/).
- We do **not** send your name, email address or phone number to these
  advertising platforms.
- **Legal basis:** consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); California treatment is on
  the [United States page](../../jurisdictions/united-states/).

## Performance and Error Monitoring

This site loads **Datadog**’s browser monitoring. It is operational
telemetry, so it does not wait for the consent banner, and declining the
banner does not stop it: it measures how quickly a page loads and records
the errors it hits, so that we can keep the pages working.

- **What Datadog receives:** the page addresses you visit and the page
  that referred you, load and resource timings, your clicks (which
  element, never anything you type), script errors and failed requests,
  your browser, device and operating system, and your IP address, from
  which it derives an approximate location. Nothing links a session to
  your name or email. No session is recorded as a replay, with one
  exception: if a page fails to render correctly, Datadog records that
  session as a replay so we can see what went wrong. A replay shows the
  page as it appeared to you, and the contents of form fields are masked.
- **Cookie:** one first-party session cookie, `_dd_s_v2`, holding a random
  session identifier that lapses after 15 minutes of inactivity (4 hours
  at most).
- **Role and retention:** Datadog acts as our **processor**, on our
  instructions, and keeps the data in the United States for no more than
  **30 days**, under the safeguards on the
  [transfers page](../../handling/transfers/); see
  [processors and platforms](../../handling/processors/) and
  [retention](../../handling/retention/).
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the pages working
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you may [object](../../rights/your-rights/) at any
  time.

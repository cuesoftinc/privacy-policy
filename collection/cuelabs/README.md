# cuelabs.cuesoft.io: Open-source Internships

## Application form

- **What:** name, email, phone, GitHub username, portfolio link, the
  discipline and duration you applied for, university, course, level,
  country, start availability, your reasons for applying, and the CV you
  upload, which is optional. We also record when you accepted the terms.
  The form preselects your country from the one Cloudflare’s edge places
  you in, as a starting point you can change; nothing is stored until you
  submit.
- **Why:** to assess and process internship applications.
- **Where:** the application, with your CV, is saved first in Cuesoft’s own
  back end (the Cueprise™ service that runs the applicant portal below),
  which is the record of your application. A copy is then written to
  Airtable, our forms database, with the CV attached; if that copy fails,
  your application still stands.
- **Legal basis:** steps at your request prior to a contract
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Confirmation:** we email you a receipt with your CV attached, copied
  to [cuelabs@cuesoft.io](mailto:cuelabs@cuesoft.io): see
  [what every site collects](../all-sites/).

Application decisions are made by humans.

## Public GitHub data

The site displays contributors to our open-source products using GitHub’s
public API: information those contributors have already made public on
GitHub, shown with attribution. **Purpose:** crediting contributors and
showing the provenance of our open-source work. **Legal basis:** our
legitimate interest in accurate attribution (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You may
[object](../../rights/your-rights/): email
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and we will remove your public
GitHub activity from our contributor displays.

## Applicant portal and sign-in

After you apply, the site has a signed-in area where you follow your
application and, once you are a member, your work. You do not sign in on
this site: sign-in happens on [account.cuesoft.io](../account/), which
returns you here.

- **What:** what the portal shows you comes from Cuesoft’s back end: your
  application and its status, your interview time, format and interviewers,
  your contract and onboarding steps, and, for members, a dashboard of your
  projects, pull requests and contributions, and a leaderboard. The
  leaderboard is seen only by members and lists members by name with their
  commit and merged pull request counts. Members with the right permission can also
  create and edit projects. On the contract page you confirm that you have
  signed; the signing link itself is a Google Docs or DocuSign link that
  Cuesoft emails you, and what you do there is governed by that service’s
  own policy.
- **Bank details and KYC:** not entered on this site. The portal sends you
  to your Cuesoft account on account.cuesoft.io for them: see
  [that page](../account/).
- **Cookie:** the portal reads your records with a session cookie that
  Cuesoft’s back end sets for you (`cueprise_session`, readable only by the
  server, not by scripts on the page). The site keeps no sign-in token in
  your browser’s storage.
- **Why:** to run the programme you applied to or were admitted to.
- **Legal basis:** steps at your request prior to a contract, and then
  performance of the participation agreement you sign (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

- **What they receive:** a cookie or device identifier, your IP address,
  the page URLs you visit on this site and the referring URL, plus basic
  device and browser metadata and the page-view events themselves. Google
  Analytics uses the IP address in passing to derive an approximate
  location and then discards it: the address itself is not stored, and the
  location is what is kept. Google Analytics keeps
  this for **14 months**: see [retention](../../handling/retention/). We
  do not send your form contents to any of them.
- **One event beyond page views:**
  when you submit an application,
  **Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and X** are told that it happened, so
  they can credit the ad you arrived from. Google Analytics is not: it
  receives the page views described above and nothing more. The event
  records the fact of the application and nothing else: not your name, not
  your email, not your phone number, and not what you wrote.
- **If you are signed in to Google:** we have Google signals switched on,
  so where you are signed in to a Google account that has Ads
  Personalisation enabled, Google may connect this visit to that account
  and combine it with what Google already holds there: location, search
  history, YouTube history and activity on sites that partner with Google.
  What that does, precisely: it lets us see age, gender and interest
  summaries, and it lets Google build advertising audiences that can reach
  you across your devices. It does **not** merge your devices together in
  our own reports (since February 2024 Google signals is not part of how
  Analytics counts users), so we do not see your phone and your laptop as
  one person. And Google uses the visit for its own ads personalisation as
  well as for our measurement. You control it on Google’s side,
  independently of us, at
  [myadcenter.google.com](https://myadcenter.google.com) and
  [Google’s activity controls](https://myaccount.google.com/activitycontrols):
  switching Ads Personalisation off there stops it for every site, not
  just ours. Declining our banner prevents it entirely.
- **Decline and none of them load.** The site works identically either
  way.
- **Withdraw any time** via the site’s **Cookie preferences** link.
  Withdrawal stops the tags loading from that point on; it does not delete
  cookies already set (you can clear those in your browser). What happens
  to data already collected differs by provider: Google Analytics holds it
  as our **processor**, on our instructions, so the
  [rights page](../../rights/your-rights/) reaches it. The exception is what
  Google signals feeds into Google’s own ads personalisation, which Google
  controls rather than us: manage that in your
  [Google account](https://myadcenter.google.com). The four advertising
  platforms hold what they collected as **independent businesses** under
  their own policies: see
  [processors and platforms](../../handling/processors/).
- An opt-out preference signal (**Global Privacy Control**) overrides a
  stored opt-in for as long as your browser sends it.
- **Legal basis:** consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); California treatment is on
  the [United States page](../../jurisdictions/united-states/).

## Performance and error monitoring

This site loads **Datadog**’s browser monitoring. It is operational
telemetry, so it does not wait for the consent banner, and declining the
banner does not stop it: it measures how quickly a page loads and records
the errors it hits, so that we can keep the pages working.

- **What Datadog receives:** the page addresses you visit and the page
  that referred you, load and resource timings, your clicks (which
  element, never anything you type), script errors and failed requests,
  your browser, device and operating system, and your IP address, from
  which it derives an approximate location. Nothing links a session to
  your name or email. No session is recorded as a replay, with one
  exception: if a page fails to render correctly, Datadog records that
  session as a replay so we can see what went wrong. A replay shows the
  page as it appeared to you, and the contents of form fields are masked.
- **Cookie:** one first-party session cookie, `_dd_s_v2`, holding a random
  session identifier that lapses after 15 minutes of inactivity (4 hours
  at most).
- **Role and retention:** Datadog acts as our **processor**, on our
  instructions, and keeps the data in the United States for no more than
  **30 days**, under the safeguards on the
  [transfers page](../../handling/transfers/); see
  [processors and platforms](../../handling/processors/) and
  [retention](../../handling/retention/).
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the pages working
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you may [object](../../rights/your-rights/) at any
  time.

## What you have to give, and what happens if you do not

- **The application form:** first name, last name, email, phone, GitHub
  username, university, course, level, country, the discipline and the
  duration you want are required, and so is ticking that you accept the terms.
  Your portfolio link, your earliest start date, your reasons for applying and
  your CV are optional. If you leave a required item out, the form does not
  send, and without an application we cannot assess you. If you leave the
  optional items out, your application is assessed without them.
- **Special categories:** the form has no field for health, religion,
  ethnicity or any other special category of data, and we ask you not to put
  any in your reasons or your CV.
- **The portal:** it shows you what Cuesoft’s back end holds about your
  application and, later, your work. Bank details, for your data allowance or
  contractor payments, and identity documents, to finish setting you up, are
  given on [account.cuesoft.io](../account/); without them we cannot pay you
  or finish setting you up.
- **The community:** joining the Discord community is your choice and is not
  needed to apply.
- **Banner:** if you decline it, the analytics and advertising tags do not
  load, and the site works identically either way.

## Who receives it, where it goes and how long it stays

- **Who receives it:** the Cuesoft back end that runs the portal and holds your
  application; Airtable, which holds the copy; Brevo, which sends the
  confirmation; Cloudflare, which tells us your country; and the Cuesoft
  staff who assess applications and run the programme. Google Docs or
  DocuSign receives what you do on the contract link we email you, under its
  own policy, and Discord receives what you share in the community under its
  own policy. Google, Meta, LinkedIn and X receive tag data only if you press
  **Accept**. Each processor is named on
  [processors and platforms](../../handling/processors/). The leaderboard is
  seen only by members. We do not sell personal data.
- **Where it goes:** some of these providers are outside your country, and the
  safeguards that cover each transfer are on
  [international transfers](../../handling/transfers/).
- **How long it stays:** records of unsuccessful or inactive applications are
  deleted or anonymised within 24 months of last contact, unless a legal duty
  requires longer, and an intern’s personnel record is kept for the engagement
  and six years after: see [retention](../../handling/retention/).

## Your rights on this site

You have the rights listed on [your rights](../../rights/your-rights/):
access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawing
consent and complaint. To use one, email
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and say that it is about CueLABS™.
Either Cuesoft entity can act on it, and we reply within one month: see
[how to exercise them](../../rights/your-rights/#how-to-exercise-them). A
request reaches both the record in our back end and the copy in Airtable.

- **Without writing to us:** sign in to your Cuesoft account, where the
  [Privacy & Data page](../account/#your-privacy-settings) lets you download
  your data and ask for your account to be deleted, and the profile, work
  history and payout pages let you correct what they hold.
- **Complaints:** you may complain to your supervisory authority at any time:
  see the [Nigeria](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/),
  [EU and UK](../../jurisdictions/eu-uk/) and
  [United States](../../jurisdictions/united-states/) pages.

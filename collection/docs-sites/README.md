# handbook.cuesoft.io, privacy.cuesoft.io, terms.cuesoft.io

The documentation sites (the company handbook and these legal pages) are
static documentation served via **GitHub Pages**, so GitHub’s
infrastructure logs apply, per
[GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

- **No forms.** These sites load Google Analytics only after you accept, and
  carry no advertising tags.
- **Cookieless analytics.** These sites also use Cloudflare Web Analytics,
  as described on [what every site collects](../all-sites/#cookieless-analytics):
  no cookies, no persistent identifiers, no fingerprinting and no cross-site
  tracking, and it runs whatever you choose.
- **Performance and error monitoring.** On their live addresses these sites
  load **Datadog**’s browser monitoring. It is operational telemetry, so it
  does not wait for a consent banner: it measures how quickly a page loads
  and records the errors it hits, so that we can keep the pages working.
  - **What Datadog receives:** the page addresses you visit and the page
    that referred you, load and resource timings, your clicks (which
    element, never anything you type), script errors, your browser, device
    and operating system, and your IP address, from which it derives an
    approximate location. Nothing links a session to your name or email, and
    no session is recorded as a replay.
  - **Cookie:** one first-party session cookie, `_dd_s_v2`, holding a random
    session identifier that lapses after 15 minutes of inactivity (4 hours
    at most).
  - **Role and retention:** Datadog acts as our **processor**, on our
    instructions, and keeps the data in the United States for no more than
    **30 days**, under the safeguards on the
    [transfers page](../../handling/transfers/); see
    [processors and platforms](../../handling/processors/) and
    [retention](../../handling/retention/).
  - **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the pages working
    (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you may [object](../../rights/your-rights/) at any
    time.
- Your **theme preference** (light or dark) is stored locally in your own
  browser and never sent to us.
- Fonts are **self-hosted**: viewing these pages sends no request to any
  font service.

## What you have to give, and what happens if you do not

Nothing is required of you. You can read these sites without giving us
anything, and there is no account, form or chat on them. If you decline or
ignore Google Analytics, it does not load and the sites read the same. If you
do not want your theme preference stored, clear your browser’s site data.

## The legal basis for each thing these sites do

| What happens                             | Legal basis                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GitHub Pages infrastructure logs         | Set by GitHub, as the host, under its privacy statement linked above |
| Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless)    | Legitimate interest in understanding site usage: GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)   |
| Datadog performance and error monitoring | Legitimate interest in keeping the pages working: Art. 6(1)(f)       |
| Google Analytics, after you accept       | Consent: Art. 6(1)(a), withdrawable at any time                      |
| Theme preference                         | Stored only in your own browser, so it is not processing by us       |

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) the same activities rest on
the matching bases in s. 25: legitimate interest where we rely on it above, and
consent for Google Analytics: see
[Nigeria](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/#lawful-bases). Where we rely on
legitimate interests, we have balanced them against your rights, and you may
[object](../../rights/your-rights/) at any time.

## Who receives it, where it goes and how long it stays

- **Who receives it:** GitHub, which hosts the sites; Cloudflare, for the
  cookieless analytics; Datadog, for the monitoring described above; and
  Google, for Google Analytics, only if you accept. Each is named on
  [processors and platforms](../../handling/processors/). We do not sell
  personal data.
- **Where it goes:** some of these providers are outside your country, and the
  safeguards that cover each transfer are on
  [international transfers](../../handling/transfers/).
- **How long it stays:** Datadog data no more than 30 days and Google
  Analytics event data 14 months: see
  [retention](../../handling/retention/).

## Your rights on these sites

You have the rights listed on [your rights](../../rights/your-rights/):
access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawing
consent and complaint. To use one, email
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and say that it is about the
documentation sites. Either Cuesoft entity can act on it, and we reply within
one month: see
[how to exercise them](../../rights/your-rights/#how-to-exercise-them). You
may complain to your supervisory authority at any time: see the
[Nigeria](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/), [EU and UK](../../jurisdictions/eu-uk/)
and [United States](../../jurisdictions/united-states/) pages.

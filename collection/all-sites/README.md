# What Every Site Collects

The following applies to **cuesoft.io**, **cueta.cuesoft.io**,
**cuelabs.cuesoft.io**, **cuehire.cuesoft.io**, **cueprise.cuesoft.io**,
**storefront.cuesoft.io** and **cuelearn.cuesoft.io** alike. The cookieless
analytics, monitoring and advertising tags sections below also cover
[clients.cuesoft.io](../clients/) and [account.cuesoft.io](../account/).

## Server and security logs

Our sites are served through Cloudflare (edge security and delivery) and
hosted on Google Firebase App Hosting. Standard technical data (IP
address, browser, requested pages, timestamps) appears in short-lived
infrastructure logs used for security, abuse prevention and reliability.

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in operating secure websites
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Retention:** no more than 30 days: see [retention](../../handling/retention/).

## Cookieless analytics

On every website, the [documentation sites](../docs-sites/),
[clients.cuesoft.io](../clients/) and [account.cuesoft.io](../account/)
included, we measure aggregate traffic with Cloudflare Web Analytics, a script that
uses **no cookies**, no persistent identifiers, no fingerprinting and no
cross-site tracking. It tells us page views and performance, not who you
are.

## Performance and error monitoring

Every website loads **Datadog**’s browser monitoring: the seven above,
[clients.cuesoft.io](../clients/) and [account.cuesoft.io](../account/). It is operational
telemetry, so it does not wait for the consent banner, and declining the
banner does not stop it: it measures how quickly a page loads and records
the errors it hits, so that we can keep the pages working. Each site’s own
page states what Datadog receives there, the one cookie it sets, how long the
data is kept and the legal basis:
[CueTA™](../cueta/#performance-and-error-monitoring),
[CueLABS™](../cuelabs/#performance-and-error-monitoring),
[CueHIRE™](../cuehire/#performance-and-error-monitoring),
[Cueprise™](../../cueprise/#performance-and-error-monitoring),
[Storefront™](../storefront/#performance-and-error-monitoring),
[Cuelearn™](../../cuelearn/#performance-and-error-monitoring),
[cuesoft.io](../cuesoft/#performance-and-error-monitoring),
[Client Work](../clients/#performance-and-error-monitoring) and
[account.cuesoft.io](../account/#performance-and-error-monitoring).

## Ace, the assistant

If you chat with Ace, the messages you type are processed by our AI
platform provider to generate an answer.

- Conversations live in your browser session and are not linked to an
  account.
- Under our agreement with our AI provider, chat inputs are **not used to
  train AI models**.
- Please do not submit sensitive personal data through the chat.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in providing the assistant you
  choose to use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

## Form acknowledgement email

Submit a form on **CueLABS™**, **CueHIRE™**, **Cueprise™**,
**Storefront™** or **Cuelearn™** and we email you a confirmation. It is copied to the department that will handle it, and
replies go there too, because the message is sent from an address nobody
reads.

We send it through **Brevo**, our email provider, which receives your name
and email address for that purpose, and on CueLABS™ the CV you attached,
which travels with the message.

Brevo also records **when you open the message and which links in it you
click**. We use that to tell whether confirmations are arriving and being
read.

- **Legal basis:** sending the confirmation rests on the same basis as the
  form itself, set out on each site’s page. The open and click measurement
  rests on our legitimate interest in knowing our own mail reaches people
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): you may
  [object](../../rights/your-rights/) at any time.
- The message loads images from our sites and from Brevo. Blocking remote
  images in your mail client prevents the open measurement.
- **Retention:** see [retention](../../handling/retention/).

## What no site does

Nine websites (CueTA™, CueLABS™, CueHIRE™, Cueprise™, Storefront™,
Cuelearn™, cuesoft.io, clients.cuesoft.io and account.cuesoft.io) carry analytics and advertising tags, and on every one of them those tags
are **gated behind a consent banner**: until you press
Accept, the site sets **no advertising or analytics cookies** and loads
**no advertising scripts and no third-party analytics** beyond the cookieless
Cloudflare measurement and the performance and error monitoring above. One
further exception, on cuesoft.io only: the Spotify player
for The CueShow™ loads if you click it and may then set Spotify’s own
cookies, which the banner does not govern: see
[cuesoft.io](../cuesoft/). Each website’s own page lists exactly which tags it
loads and who receives the data: [CueTA™](../cueta/),
[CueLABS™](../cuelabs/), [CueHIRE™](../cuehire/),
[Cueprise™](../../cueprise/), [Storefront™](../storefront/),
[Cuelearn™](../../cuelearn/), [cuesoft.io](../cuesoft/),
[Client Work](../clients/) and [account.cuesoft.io](../account/). The [documentation sites](../docs-sites/) carry
none of them, only the cookieless analytics above and the performance and
error monitoring described on their page. The CueTA™ website takes payment for
enrolment: see [CueTA™](../cueta/#enrolment-and-payment). Cards are also saved on
the signed-in Payment Methods page of [account.cuesoft.io](../account/#cards),
where the card is typed only into Paystack’s own window and Cuesoft never
receives the card number or security code, and the signed-in portal of
[CueHIRE™](../cuehire/#paying-an-invoice) pays by card on Paystack’s own pages
and window: see those pages for what is asked.
No other website asks for payment data.

### Names and lifetimes

These are the names and lifetimes of the entries the table above
describes. A lifetime is how long your browser keeps the entry; you can
clear any of them sooner in your browser.

**Your consent choice.** Each of the nine websites with these tags keeps
your choice in your browser’s local storage, under a name of its own:
`cuesoft-consent`, `cueta-consent`, `cuelabs-consent`, `cuehire-consent`,
`cueprise-consent`, `cuelearn-consent`, `storefront-consent`,
`clients-consent` and `account-consent`. Local storage has no expiry, so
the entry stays until you change your choice through **Cookie
preferences** or clear the site’s data. If you decline, the site also
sets a cookie of the same name that lasts one year. That cookie only ever
records a decline, never an accept.

**Cookies set after you accept.** On the live sites, the tags load Google
Analytics, Google Ads, and the Meta, LinkedIn and X pixels. The cookies
below are the ones those tags can set.

| Google Ads       | `_gcl_au`; and `_gcl_aw` after a click on one of our Google ads                                                            | 90 days each                                                                                                                                                         |

### Which ones need your consent

The analytics and advertising cookies and identifiers need your consent, and
the banner on each of the nine websites with these tags is how you give it
(GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): see [Cookies and ePrivacy](../../jurisdictions/eu-uk/#cookies-and-eprivacy).
The Datadog cookie is operational telemetry and does not wait for the banner,
and the sign-in cookies are what keep you signed in to a service you asked for.
The Spotify player is covered by your click.

### How to manage them

- **Cookie preferences.** Use the link in the footer of the site where you
  accepted to withdraw your consent. Withdrawal stops the tags loading from
  that point on; it does not delete cookies already set, which you can
  clear in your browser. Each site keeps its own choice.
- **Global Privacy Control.** If your browser sends it, no analytics or
  advertising tag loads at all, even if you accepted before. See the
  [United States page](../../jurisdictions/united-states/).
- **Your browser.** You can block or delete cookies and site data in your
  browser settings at any time. Signed-in products need their session
  cookie to keep you signed in.
- **Google’s controls.** What Google signals feeds into Google’s own ads
  personalisation is managed in your Google account, at
  [myadcenter.google.com](https://myadcenter.google.com) and
  [Google’s activity controls](https://myaccount.google.com/activitycontrols).
- **Spotify.** Clear Spotify’s cookies in your browser and do not click the
  player again.
- **Deletion and objection.** To have data collected through these
  technologies deleted, or to object, use the
  [rights page](../../rights/your-rights/).

If we add or change a cookie, we update this section and the site’s own
page, and the effective date on the [main page](../../) changes with it.

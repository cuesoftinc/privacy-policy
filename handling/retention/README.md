# Retention

We keep personal data only as long as the purpose it was collected for
requires, then delete or anonymise it.

What Google signals contributes to Google’s own ads personalisation sits outside that: we set no period for it and cannot delete it, because it is held against your Google account rather than in our property. You control both how long Google keeps that activity and whether it is collected at all, through [Google’s activity controls](https://myaccount.google.com/activitycontrols), which offer auto-delete after 3, 18 or 36 months, and through [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com) |

Verified deletion requests are honoured sooner: see
[your rights](../../rights/your-rights/). Where law requires us to keep a
record longer (for example tax or dispute records), we keep only what that
law requires, for only as long as it requires.

When a Cuelearn™ licensing agreement ends, the Institution can ask for an
export of its data within **90 days**, and the data is then deleted, unless
the agreement says otherwise. No timer deletes it: an expired licence stops
sign-in and the use of licensed modules, and Cuesoft deletes the data once
the 90 days have passed. A file that is deleted is kept in storage for 7
more days, then removed for good. The database is backed up automatically by
its provider, which keeps a single backup for disaster recovery, so deleted
data stays in it only until the next backup replaces it.

Cuelearn™ does not record live sessions or invigilated exams. It creates
each session as a Google Meet and turns on no recording or transcript. A
teacher can add a link to a recording made in Google Meet, and the platform
keeps only that link. It does not keep a copy of the weekly report it sends
a parent or guardian, a PDF drawn when it is sent; it keeps a record of each
delivery (the learner, the week, the channel and whether it was sent). It keeps a certificate’s record of the
holder, course, code, date and whether it is valid. No timer deletes the
link, a delivery record or a certificate record: each stays until it is
deleted at the Institution’s instruction.

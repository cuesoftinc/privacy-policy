# Retention

We keep personal data only as long as the purpose it was collected for
requires, then delete or anonymise it.

| Data                                                                                                          | Retention                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Form submissions (applications, enquiries)                                                                    | Kept while we process and follow up; records of unsuccessful or inactive applications and enquiries are deleted or anonymised within **24 months** of last contact, unless a legal duty requires longer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Infrastructure and security logs                                                                              | No more than **30 days**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Website and documentation-site performance and error monitoring (Datadog)                                     | No more than **30 days**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Google Analytics, where you opted in                                                                          | Event-level data: **14 months**, the longest Google Analytics allows, after which Google deletes it. Aggregated reports have no expiry, and identify nobody. Withdrawing consent stops new collection; a deletion request through the [rights page](../../rights/your-rights/) reaches what was already collected, because Analytics holds it as our processor. What Google signals contributes to Google’s own ads personalisation sits outside that: we set no period for it and cannot delete it, because it is held against your Google account rather than in our property. You control both how long Google keeps that activity and whether it is collected at all, through [Google’s activity controls](https://myaccount.google.com/activitycontrols), which offer auto-delete after 3, 18 or 36 months, and through [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com) |
| Acknowledgement email                                                                                         | Delivery log: **1 month**, then deleted. Message preview: **never stored**: the provider keeps no copy of what the message said. Open and click records: retained by the provider **without a fixed period**. We copy none of it into our own systems                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Ace conversations                                                                                             | Held in your browser session; server-side processing is transient                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Cohort/programme communication                                                                                | For the life of the programme relationship, then per the signed agreement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| CueTA™ enrolment, order and invoice records                                                                   | Kept while your enrolment and subscription run; invoice and tax records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and no longer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Account site records (account.cuesoft.io: profile, sessions, saved cards, notification choices, export files) | Kept while your account is open. Ending a session ends it at once. A saved card you remove is marked deleted, not erased, and a card you save is never stored as a number or security code. An export file can be downloaded for **seven days**. When you ask to delete your account it is booked for **30 days** later, so you can cancel; then your saved cards, notification choices and export files are deleted and the account is marked deleted, which stops sign-in but does not erase the record                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Payout details, identity documents and work history (account.cuesoft.io)                                      | Held with your personnel record on our platform, so for as long as that record: see the next row. Deleting your account does not remove them: ask on the [rights page](../../rights/your-rights/)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Personnel records (contractors, interns and staff)                                                            | The engagement, then **six years** from its end: the record-keeping window Nigerian tax and company law set. Set out in the [personnel privacy notice](https://handbook.cuesoft.io/policies/personnel-privacy/)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Apparule capture photos                                                                                       | Unsaved or failed captures: **24 hours**. Saved captures: **30 days** from saving; the measurements stay until you delete them. See the [Apparule notice](../../apparule/#body-photos-and-measurements)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Apparule order messages and notifications                                                                     | Thread messages: **30 days** after the order closes. In-app notifications: **90 days**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Expendit uploaded files                                                                                       | Deleted within **1 hour** of being read or discarded; the import record **90 days** after you confirm or discard it. See the [Expendit notice](../../expendit/#importing-statements-and-receipts)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Storage provider recovery copies (Apparule and Expendit files)                                                | Up to **7 days** after deletion                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Upstat visitor events                                                                                         | Individual events **90 days**; daily totals, which identify nobody, **13 months**; the daily value behind unique-visitor counts is destroyed **48 hours** after the day ends. See the [Upstat notice](../../upstat/#for-visitors-to-sites-that-use-upstat)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Upstat check results                                                                                          | **90 days**; daily uptime figures **13 months**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Apparule and Expendit account data, books and measurements                                                    | Until you delete them or your account; Apparule’s order and payment records are kept, without your name or email, for as long as tax and company law require                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Data under signed engagements (including Cueprise™ licensee data)                                             | Per the engagement’s terms and applicable law                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

Verified deletion requests are honoured sooner: see
[your rights](../../rights/your-rights/). Where law requires us to keep a
record longer (for example tax or dispute records), we keep only what that
law requires, for only as long as it requires.

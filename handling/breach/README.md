# Breach Notification

If a breach of security leads to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss,
alteration or unauthorised disclosure of personal data, we will:

1. **Promptly assess and contain it** — establishing what data, whose, and
   what risk.
2. **Notify the competent authorities where and when required:**
   - the **NDPC within 72 hours** of becoming aware, where the NDPA
     applies — see [Nigeria](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/);
   - **EU/UK supervisory authorities** without undue delay and, where
     feasible, within 72 hours, where the GDPR or UK GDPR requires
     notification — see [EU/UK](../../jurisdictions/eu-uk/);
   - US authorities and affected residents as state breach-notification
     laws require — see [United States](../../jurisdictions/united-states/).
3. **Inform affected people** without undue delay where their rights and
   freedoms are at material risk, with what happened, what we did, and
   what they can do.
4. **For Cueprise™ licensee data**, notify the affected licensee without
   undue delay with the detail it needs for its own obligations — see the
   [Cueprise™ Privacy Notice](../../cueprise/).

We keep an internal record of every incident, including those that did not
meet a notification threshold, and what we changed afterwards.

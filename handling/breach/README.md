# Breach Notification

If a breach of security leads to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss,
alteration or unauthorised disclosure of personal data, we will:

1. **Promptly assess and contain it**: establishing what data, whose, and
   what risk.
2. **Notify the competent authorities where and when required:**
   - the **NDPC within 72 hours** of becoming aware, where the NDPA
     applies: see [Nigeria](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/);
   - **EU/UK supervisory authorities** without undue delay and, where
     feasible, within 72 hours, where the GDPR or UK GDPR requires
     notification: see [EU/UK](../../jurisdictions/eu-uk/);
   - US authorities and affected residents as state breach-notification
     laws require: see [United States](../../jurisdictions/united-states/).
3. **Inform affected people** without undue delay where their rights and
   freedoms are at material risk, with what happened, what we did, and
   what they can do.
4. **For Cueprise™ licensee data**, notify the affected licensee without
   undue delay with the detail it needs for its own obligations: see the
   [Cueprise™ Privacy Notice](../../cueprise/).
5. **For Cuelearn™ Institution data**, notify the affected Institution
   without undue delay, with the detail it needs for its own obligations to
   its authority, its Learners and their parents or guardians: see the
   [Cuelearn™ Privacy Notice](../../cuelearn/#security-and-breach).

We keep an internal record of every incident, including those that did not
meet a notification threshold, and what we changed afterwards.

## When a processor tells us

If one of our [processors](../processors/) has a breach that touches personal
data we are responsible for, we treat it from the moment we learn of it as
our own: we assess it, contain it and notify as above.

Each processor’s own data processing terms set the period within which it
must tell us of a breach. Most say “without undue delay” or “promptly” and
no more. Five name a limit in hours:

| Processor                                         | Period it owes us                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Anthropic                                         | Without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours                                |
| Certifier                                         | Immediately, and no later than 36 hours after it learns of the breach                |
| Airtable                                          | Without undue delay, and no later than 72 hours after it substantiates the breach    |
| Cloudinary                                        | Without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours                                |
| Paystack                                          | Without undue delay, and no later than 72 hours after it becomes aware of the breach |
| Cloudflare                                        | Without undue delay                                                                  |
| Brevo                                             | Without undue delay                                                                  |
| Datadog                                           | Without undue delay                                                                  |
| GitHub                                            | Without undue delay                                                                  |
| Aiven                                             | Without undue delay                                                                  |
| Meta (WhatsApp Business)                          | Without undue delay                                                                  |
| QuickBooks (Intuit)                               | Without undue delay                                                                  |
| Google (Cloud, Workspace, Firebase and Analytics) | Promptly and without undue delay                                                     |
| Lark                                              | Promptly                                                                             |
| ClickHouse                                        | Promptly                                                                             |
| Groq                                              | Not applicable: it receives no personal data                                         |

## What we tell you

Where we inform you, we say in plain words:

- what happened and when;
- what kind of data is involved, and roughly how many people are affected;
- what the likely consequences are for you;
- what we have done and are doing about it; and
- what you can do to protect yourself, and who to contact:
  [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).

## When we are the processor

For data on a licensed platform, the Institution or Licensee is the
controller. It decides whether to notify its authority and the people
affected, and we give it what it needs to do so and help it.

## If you think there has been a breach

Email [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) with what you saw. If you
think your Cuesoft account has been used by someone else, end every session
other than your own on its Security page: see
[security](../security/#what-you-can-do).

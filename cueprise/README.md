# Cueprise™ Privacy Notice

**Effective date: 22 November 2026**

This notice covers **Cueprise™** in both the places you meet it: the
website at **cueprise.cuesoft.io**, where you can read about the product and
ask for a demo, and the **platform** itself, licensed to organisations
("licensees") under written agreements.

Those two halves work differently, which is why they are kept apart below. On
the website, Cuesoft is the controller of what you send us, in the ordinary
way. Inside a licensed deployment, **the licensee, not Cuesoft, is the data
controller** of the data the platform holds.

## The Cueprise™ website

  the list, and anything you added in the message box. We also record the

### Performance and error monitoring

This site loads **Datadog**’s browser monitoring. It is operational
telemetry, so it does not wait for the consent banner, and declining the
banner does not stop it: it measures how quickly a page loads and records
the errors it hits, so that we can keep the pages working.

- **What Datadog receives:** the page addresses you visit and the page
  that referred you, load and resource timings, your clicks (which
  element, never anything you type), script errors and failed requests,
  your browser, device and operating system, and your IP address, from
  which it derives an approximate location. Nothing links a session to
  your name or email. No session is recorded as a replay, with one
  exception: if a page fails to render correctly, Datadog records that
  session as a replay so we can see what went wrong. A replay shows the
  page as it appeared to you, and the contents of form fields are masked.
- **Cookie:** one first-party session cookie, `_dd_s_v2`, holding a random
  session identifier that lapses after 15 minutes of inactivity (4 hours
  at most).
- **Role and retention:** Datadog acts as our **processor**, on our
  instructions, and keeps the data in the United States for no more than
  **30 days**, under the safeguards on the
  [transfers page](../handling/transfers/); see
  [processors and platforms](../handling/processors/) and
  [retention](../handling/retention/).
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the pages working
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you may [object](../rights/your-rights/) at any
  time.

## The Cueprise™ platform

Everything from here describes a licensed deployment rather than the website.

### How Cueprise™ is deployed

Every licensee gets its **own isolated deployment with its own database**.
No licensee’s data shares an instance, a database or an encryption
boundary with any other licensee’s. Deployments are operated by Cuesoft on
Google Cloud by default, or on the licensee’s own cloud (AWS, Azure or Google
Cloud) or on-premise where the licensing agreement says so.

A deployment periodically verifies its licence key against Cuesoft’s
licensing service. The request payload carries the licence key and nothing
else; like any web request it also reveals its source IP address, which the
licensing service processes transiently for rate limiting, as described
under [service telemetry](#service-telemetry) below.

### Lawful basis and the licensee’s duties

The [Cueprise™ Terms](https://terms.cuesoft.io/cueprise/) govern how the
platform may be used. For licensee data, the licensee is the controller,
so these duties are the licensee’s:

- **A lawful basis for each activity.** The licensee chooses and records
  the basis for what it does with the data (GDPR Art. 6 and the NDPA s. 25),
  and Cuesoft does not choose it. The licensee tells its staff, customers
  and shoppers what it does, in its own privacy notice.
- **Required or optional.** The licensee decides which details its people
  must give, and tells them whether each one is required or optional.
- **Special-category and sensitive data.** Some records can reveal more
  than they first seem to. A leave record can show a kind of leave such as
  sick leave, which may reveal health, and notes, files and pictures can
  hold anything. The licensee decides whether to record such data. It is
  responsible for the condition that allows it (GDPR Art. 9 and the NDPA’s
  rules on sensitive personal data). Cuesoft processes it only on the
  licensee’s instructions, like all other licensee data.
- **Children.** The platform is built for businesses, their staff and their
  customers. Cuesoft does not direct it to children. A licensee that
  collects data about a child is responsible for the parent’s or guardian’s
  consent: see [children](../rights/children/).
- **Marketing messages.** The platform adds a customer’s email address and
  phone number to the licensee’s newsletter and WhatsApp update lists when
  the customer record is created, and records when each is subscribed or
  unsubscribed. The licensee is responsible for the legal basis for those
  messages and for honouring every unsubscribe.
- **Decisions about people.** Cuesoft makes no decision about any person
  from licensee data. Where a licensee uses platform results (such as pay
  calculations, loyalty rules or credit terms) to decide something about
  its own people or customers, the licensee is responsible for human review
  and for the rules on automated decisions that apply to it.
- **Integrations.** The provider behind each integration a licensee
  connects is the licensee’s own service provider, under the licensee’s own
  arrangement with it, and is not Cuesoft’s sub-processor.

### Payments

Card details **never enter Cueprise™**. Online payments happen on
**Paystack’s** hosted checkout page under Paystack’s own security
certification; the platform sends Paystack only the payer’s email address,
the amount and an order reference, and receives back the payment status.

Where a form takes bank details, you pick the bank from Paystack’s list and
type the account number. The platform sends Paystack the bank and the account
number to verify them, and Paystack returns the account name for you to
confirm.

### AI features

Ace, the assistant inside a Cueprise™ deployment, and the picture tools and
drafted content that come with it, run on Google’s Gemini models on Vertex AI.
Picture upscaling uses Google’s Imagen.

- **What is sent:** your prompt, the record context needed to answer it, and
  any pictures you choose to attach or edit.
- **Training:** none of it is used to train models.
- **Your confirmation:** Ace takes an action only after you confirm it.
- **Who is involved:** Google acts as a sub-processor for this, listed in the
  [table below](#sub-processors).

### AI output and your responsibilities

AI output can be wrong, so a person should check it before relying on it.
Ace takes an action only after you confirm it. No AI feature makes a
decision with legal or similarly significant effect on its own. The
licensee, as controller, decides whether its staff use these features and
has the lawful basis for what they send. The
[Ace terms](https://terms.cuesoft.io/websites/ace/) say more about what Ace
is and is not.

For **relationship data**, the licensee contacts, contracts, invoices and

Cuesoft is the controller. **Legal basis:** where the licensee is an

Cuesoft is also the controller of the **service telemetry** needed to run

#### Service telemetry

- **Server-side**: request traces, error reports and operational logs from
  each deployment’s services.
- **Admin application**: usage analytics in the staff-facing admin app,
  including, in production, a session-replay sample of roughly one in five
  admin sessions used for debugging. Sign-in callback addresses are
  scrubbed before anything is sent, and replay is of the admin interface
  used by licensee staff; storefront shoppers are not session-replayed by
  the platform.
- **Security**: IP addresses are processed transiently to rate-limit
  sign-in and licensing endpoints and to attribute traffic through our
  edge protection.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in securing, supporting and
improving the platform (Art. 6(1)(f)). Licensee staff whose sessions
appear in telemetry are usually not parties to the licensing contract, so
we do not rely on contract performance for this processing; the
legitimate-interest balance is struck by the sampling, the scrubbing and
the admin-only scope described above.

Service telemetry, including session-replay samples and any IP addresses
appearing in request logs, is kept for **30 days**, the same period the
[retention page](../handling/retention/) sets for infrastructure and
security logs. The rate-limiting counters themselves are shorter-lived
still: they hold an IP only for the minutes their sliding window covers.
Relationship data follows the retention page and the agreement. The
[jurisdiction pages](../jurisdictions/nigeria/) apply to this data as they
do to website data.

### Cookies and browser storage in the platform

- **Session cookie:** when you sign in, the platform sets a session cookie
  that scripts on the page cannot read. It is sent only over HTTPS, only to
  the same site, and it ends when you sign out. Its lifetime is set in the
  deployment’s configuration.
- **Sign-in cookies:** during Google or workspace single sign-on, two
  short-lived cookies carry the sign-in between pages, for 10 minutes and 5
  minutes. They are removed when the sign-in finishes.
- **Browser storage:** the admin application keeps your sign-in tokens, the
  branch you work in, saved table views, the last currency you chose,
  point-of-sale settings and a few interface choices in your browser’s local
  storage, and, only during sign-in, the page to return to and the email
  you tried in session storage. This stays on your device and is not sent
  to us as analytics. You can clear it in your browser.
- **Analytics:** the usage analytics and session-replay sample in the admin
  application are described under [service telemetry](#service-telemetry).
- **Storefronts:** cookies and tags a licensee adds to its own storefront
  are the licensee’s choice, and its own notices cover them. For the
  cookies on the website, see
  [cookies and similar technologies](../collection/all-sites/#cookies-and-similar-technologies).

### Retention on the platform

- **Licensee data** is kept as the licensee instructs, under the licensing
  agreement and applicable law: see [retention](../handling/retention/).
- **A retention setting.** A deployment has a setting where the licensee

  removes.
- **Service telemetry** is kept for 30 days, as set out above.
- **On termination or expiry**, licensee data is returned or deleted as the
  agreement provides.

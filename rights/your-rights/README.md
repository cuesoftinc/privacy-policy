# Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You control your personal data. Depending on your jurisdiction (see the
[jurisdiction pages](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/)), you have the right
to:

| Right                                 | What it means                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Access**                            | A copy of the personal data we hold about you, and what we do with it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Correction**                        | Fixing inaccurate or incomplete data                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Deletion**                          | Erasure of your data where no legal duty requires keeping it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Restriction**                       | Pausing processing while a dispute about it is resolved                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Objection**                         | Objecting to processing based on legitimate interests                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Portability**                       | Your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Withdraw consent**                  | At any time, without affecting prior lawful processing: use the **Cookie preferences** link in the footer of the site where you accepted, since a choice does not carry between sites. The Spotify player on cuesoft.io is separate: the banner does not govern it, so clear Spotify’s cookies in your browser and simply do not click it again |
| **Complain**                          | To your supervisory authority: named per jurisdiction                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Rights under the NDPA**             | The data-subject rights in Part VI of the NDPA, including not to be subject to solely automated decisions: see the [Nigeria page](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/#your-rights-under-the-ndpa)                                                                                                                                                      |

On [account.cuesoft.io](../../collection/account/#your-privacy-settings), the
**Privacy & Data** page lets you download a copy of your data and ask for your
account to be deleted without writing to us, and the profile, work history and
payout pages let you correct what they hold. What that deletion does and does
not remove is set out on that page; for anything else, use the steps below.

## How to exercise them

1. Email [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) with what you want
   and enough information for us to find and verify you (usually the email
   or phone you used with us). We verify every request to protect your
   data from impostors: we ask only for what verification needs.
2. **We respond within one month.** Where a request is complex, the law
   lets us extend (up to two further months under the GDPR); if we extend,
   we tell you within the first month and say why.
3. Exercising rights is **free**, except where the law permits a
   reasonable fee for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests, and if
   we ever rely on that, we will say so in writing.
4. If we refuse a request (for example, deletion of a record the law
   requires us to keep), we tell you why and which authority you can
   complain to.
5. You may use an authorised agent where your law provides for one; we
   verify the agent’s authority and your identity.

## Automated decisions

We do not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly
significant effects. Application decisions across all programmes are made
by humans.

## Not being treated worse for exercising a right

We will never deny you a service, charge you a different price or give you a
lower quality of service because you exercised a privacy right. The
[United States page](../../jurisdictions/united-states/) says the same for
California residents, and we apply it to everyone.

## Appeals

If we refuse a request, we tell you why and which authority you can complain
to (step 4 above). Where a US state law gives you the right to appeal, tell us
you are appealing by emailing [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io). A
different reviewer decides the appeal, and we answer within the period your
state’s law sets. If we still refuse, we point you to your state attorney
general: see the [United States page](../../jurisdictions/united-states/).

## Exercising rights for a child

A parent or guardian may exercise a child’s rights for the child.

1. Email [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io), say that you are the
   child’s parent or guardian, and say whose data the request is about.
2. We verify your identity and your relationship to the child before we act,
   asking only for what verification needs, and we then follow steps 2 to 5
   above.
3. For a Cuelearn™ Learner enrolled by a school or Institution, the
   Institution is the controller of the Learner’s records, so the request
   goes to the Institution, and we refer any request we receive to it: see
   [where we are the processor](#where-we-are-the-processor) below and
   [children](../children/).

## Where we are the processor

For data on the Cuelearn™ platform and on a licensed Cueprise™ deployment,
the Institution or Licensee is the controller. Requests about that data go
to it, and we help it answer: see the
[Cuelearn™ Privacy Notice](../../cuelearn/#when-cuesoft-is-the-processor) and
the [Cueprise™ Privacy Notice](../../cueprise/#when-cuesoft-is-the-processor).
Requests about data Cuesoft controls come to us at
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).

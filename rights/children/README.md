# Children

Our websites, programmes and products are directed to people **18 and
older**, with one exception: **Apparule** can be used from the age of 13,
with a parent’s or guardian’s agreement for anyone under 18 (see the
[Apparule notice](../../apparule/#who-can-use-apparule)).

- Apart from Apparule, we do not knowingly collect personal data from
  anyone under 18, and Apparule collects none from anyone under 13.
- Our forms and programmes state the 18+ requirement, and the
  [Terms of Service](https://terms.cuesoft.io) make it a condition of
  applying.
- If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact
  [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and we will verify and
  delete it.
- Apparule asks for a parent’s or guardian’s agreement before the first
  capture of anyone under 18, which also meets the age-of-consent
  provisions of GDPR Art. 8 wherever a member state sets that age between
  13 and 16. Outside Apparule we do not direct services to children, and
  if we learn we hold a minor’s data, deletion is the remedy.

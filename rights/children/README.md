# Children

Our websites, programmes and products are directed to people **18 and
older**, with two exceptions. **Apparule** can be used from the age of 13,
with a parent’s or guardian’s agreement for anyone under 18 (see the
[Apparule notice](../../apparule/#who-can-use-apparule)). And **Cuelearn™**
learners may be under 18 where their school enrols them: the school decides
who uses the platform and is responsible for the parent’s or guardian’s
consent (see the
[Cuelearn™ notice](../../cuelearn/#the-cuelearn-platform)).

- Apart from Apparule and Cuelearn™ learners enrolled by their school, we do
  not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18, and Apparule
  collects none from anyone under 13.
- Our forms and programmes state the 18+ requirement, and the
  [Terms of Service](https://terms.cuesoft.io) make it a condition of
  applying, except for Cuelearn™ learners enrolled by their school.
- If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact
  [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and we will verify and
  delete it.
- Apparule requires a parent’s or guardian’s agreement for anyone under
  18, which also meets the age-of-consent
  provisions of GDPR Art. 8 wherever a member state sets that age between
  13 and 16. Outside Apparule and Cuelearn™ we do not direct services to children, and
  if we learn we hold a minor’s data, deletion is the remedy.

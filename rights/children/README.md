# Children

Our websites, programmes and products are directed to people **18 and
older**, with two exceptions. **Apparule** can be used from the age of 13,
with a parent’s or guardian’s agreement for anyone under 18 (see the
[Apparule notice](../../apparule/#who-can-use-apparule)). And **Cuelearn™**
learners may be under 18 where their school enrols them: the school decides
who uses the platform and is responsible for the parent’s or guardian’s
consent (see the
[Cuelearn™ notice](../../cuelearn/#the-cuelearn-platform)).

- Apart from Apparule and Cuelearn™ learners enrolled by their school, we do
  not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18, and Apparule
  collects none from anyone under 13.
- Our forms and programmes state the 18+ requirement, and the
  [Terms of Service](https://terms.cuesoft.io) make it a condition of
  applying, except for Cuelearn™ learners enrolled by their school.
- If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact
  [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and we will verify and
  delete it.
- Apparule requires a parent’s or guardian’s agreement for anyone under
  18, which also meets the age-of-consent
  provisions of GDPR Art. 8 wherever a member state sets that age between
  13 and 16. Outside Apparule and Cuelearn™ we do not direct services to children, and
  if we learn we hold a minor’s data, deletion is the remedy.

## Cuelearn™ Learners under 18

The Cuelearn™ platform at canvas.cuesoft.io is licensed to an Institution
(a school, academy, university or training provider). Where the Institution
enrols a Learner (a student the Institution gives access to) under 18:

- **The Institution decides who uses the platform.** It enrols its Learners
  and Teachers, and it is responsible for the consent of the parent or
  guardian of every Learner under 18.
- **The Institution is the controller.** It decides how a Learner’s data is
  used, and its own privacy notice governs the records about the Learner.
- **Cuesoft is the processor.** We process a Learner’s data for the
  Institution, on its instructions, under the data terms of the licensing
  agreement: see the
  [Cuelearn™ notice](../../cuelearn/#when-cuesoft-is-the-processor).
- **We do not sell personal data**, Learners’ included.
- **Parents and guardians** receive weekly reports about their Learner, by
  email and by WhatsApp.
- **Invigilated exams** run on Google Meet and the Learner’s camera must be
  on for the whole sitting: see the
  [Cuelearn™ notice](../../cuelearn/#exams).
- **AI** drafts lessons, quizzes and marking on Google Gemini, and prompts are
  not used to train models: see the
  [Cuelearn™ notice](../../cuelearn/#ai).
- **Rights.** A Learner, or a parent or guardian, makes requests to the
  Institution, and we refer any request we receive to it: see
  [exercising rights for a child](../your-rights/#exercising-rights-for-a-child).

## Our other websites and programmes

Outside Apparule and Cuelearn™ Learners enrolled by their school, our
websites, programmes and products are for people 18 and older, and we do not
direct them to children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child
has given us personal data, email [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).
We will verify and delete it, as the list above says.

## Law that applies to children’s data

- **Nigeria:** the NDPA sets special rules for a child’s data: see
  [Children and the NDPA](../../jurisdictions/nigeria/#children-and-the-ndpa).
- **EU and UK:** see [Children](../../jurisdictions/eu-uk/#children) on the
  EU and UK page.
- **United States:** see
  [Children and US privacy law](../../jurisdictions/united-states/#children-and-us-privacy-law).

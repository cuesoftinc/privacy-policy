# Children

Our websites and programmes are directed to people **18 and older**.

- We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.
- Our forms and programmes state the 18+ requirement, and the
  [Terms of Service](https://terms.cuesoft.io) make it a condition of
  applying.
- If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact
  [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and we will verify and
  delete it.
- Because we do not direct services to children, we do not knowingly
  process data subject to child-specific regimes (such as COPPA in the US
  or the age-of-consent provisions of GDPR Art. 8); if we learn we hold
  such data, deletion is the remedy.
